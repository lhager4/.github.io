Our Vision

Welcome to the world's first borderless eco-micronation. We are a global network of physically connected properties dedicated to environmental sustainability and absolute equal rights. Whether you own a massive farm or rent a single room, your space can become a sovereign enclave of our green future.

The Green Mandate & Constitution

Article I: Absolute Equality
Every citizen holds equal voting power. There are no kings, presidents, or rulers. We operate as a direct digital democracy.

Article II: The Ecological Pledge
All registered lands must actively minimize carbon footprints, reduce waste, and nurture local biodiversity.

Article III: Sovereignty of the Mind
Citizens must follow local host-nation property laws while treating their designated space as a cultural sanctuary of our nation.
